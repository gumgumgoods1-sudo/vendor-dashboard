A trade is a sale where the customer pays in cards instead of cash. The cards under You Gave are what you sold; the cards under You Got are how they paid, valued at Rate % of their market price. Cash +/− settles whatever's left over — positive means they paid you, negative means you paid them.
Profit = Paid To You − Cost Out. Identical to a cash sale: what you got for the cards, minus what those cards cost you.  Balance should read $0 — it's Market Out − Credit Given − Cash. Anything else means a price or cash figure is mistyped, not that you made or lost money.
